HEAD ADMIN INITIAL SETUP

1. Upload all replacement files to your website.
2. Publish firestore.rules in Firebase Console > Firestore Database > Rules.
3. In Firebase Console > Firestore Database > Data, open:
   admins / YOUR_UID
4. Make sure the document contains:
   uid: "YOUR_UID"
   enabled: true
   role: "head-admin"

Existing admin documents without a role continue to work as normal Administrators.
You must manually mark the first Head Administrator because no Head Administrator exists yet to grant the role in the Control Panel.

PROTECTION
- Normal Administrators cannot promote anyone to Head Administrator.
- Normal Administrators cannot demote, disable, or edit a Head Administrator account.
- Head Administrators can promote or demote other Head Administrators.
- A Head Administrator cannot change or remove their own Head Administrator role in the Control Panel.
