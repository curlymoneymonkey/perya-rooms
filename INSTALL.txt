PERYA DICE NOTIFICATION CENTER

1. Replace your current dashboard.html with the dashboard.html in this folder.
2. Keep your current dashboard.js and style.css unchanged.
3. Add notifications.js and notifications.css beside dashboard.html.
4. Deploy all four files to Netlify/GitHub.

The new dashboard.html loads:
- your existing style.css
- your existing favorites.css
- notifications.css
- your existing dashboard.js
- notifications.js
- your existing account-menu.js

Firestore requirement:
The signed-in user must be allowed to read notifications where recipientUid equals their UID, update the read field, and delete their own notification documents.

No orderBy is used, so this frontend query does not need a composite index. Notifications are sorted newest-first in the browser.
